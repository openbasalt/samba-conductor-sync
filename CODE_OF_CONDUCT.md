# Code of conduct

OpenBasalt projects adopt the [Contributor Covenant, version
2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as
their code of conduct. It applies to every repository, issue, pull request,
discussion and other space of the organization, and to anyone representing
a project.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep the
discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory
language are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers at
conduct@openbasalt.org. Reports are read by the organization's maintainers
only and handled confidentially. Maintainers who are involved in a report do
not take part in handling it.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct reports: follow the repository's
`SECURITY.md`.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban a contributor
temporarily or permanently, following the enforcement guidelines of the
Contributor Covenant 2.1.
